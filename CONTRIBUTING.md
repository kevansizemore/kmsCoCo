# Contributing & Forking kmsCoCo

Thank you for your interest in **kmsCoCo**!

---

## Fork-First Model (Issues & Discussions Disabled)

* **Issues and Discussions Are Disabled**: This repository intentionally disables GitHub Issues, Discussions, and bug tracker features.
* **Forks Are Warmly Welcomed**: Under the terms of the **GNU General Public License v3.0**, you are actively encouraged to fork this repository, apply your own bug fixes, build custom features, and share your fork with the community.
* **No Obligation to Monitor or Merge Pull Requests**: Pull requests are not monitored, reviewed, or accepted. The maintainer has **no obligation, commitment, or SLA to view, discuss, accept, or merge pull requests** or patches. Please maintain your changes and enhancements within your own independent fork.
* **Upstream Contributions**: If you develop general-purpose 6809/6309 CPU fixes, SAM/GIME hardware timings, or Dragon emulation enhancements that would benefit the broader community, we strongly recommend submitting them directly upstream to [Ciaran Anscomb's XRoar project](https://www.6809.org.uk/xroar/).

## Guidelines for Forking

When creating a public fork:
1. Ensure your fork remains compliant with the **GNU General Public License v3.0** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
2. Maintain original author copyright notices (`Copyright 2003-2026 Ciaran Anscomb`) across all source and header files as required by GPLv3 Section 5.
3. Ensure no proprietary ROM images or copyrighted binary blobs are committed to public repositories.
4. Retain appropriate attribution and disclosures.
